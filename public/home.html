Collab

Collab is a video meeting and collaboration app built by Alessandro Alghisi. Start or join a call in the browser, on desktop or on your phone, and work together while you talk.

Open Collab

What it does

  • Video and audio meetings with invite links.
  • A live shared code editor with per-person cursors and a Run button.
  • Chat, file sharing and a shared problems panel during the call.
  • Meeting scheduling with an optional two-way Google Calendar sync.

How Collab uses Google data

You can sign in with Google to create an account. Collab receives your name, email address and profile picture for that account.

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Collab requests the calendar.events permission. It adds meetings you schedule in Collab to your Google Calendar, and shows events you create or change in Google Calendar on Collab's calendar. Collab does not use this data for anything else. You can disconnect at any time.
