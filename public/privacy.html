Collab – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Collab (https://alexalghisi.github.io/collab/) is a video meeting and collaboration app built and operated by Alessandro Alghisi. This page explains what data Collab uses and why.

Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, Collab receives your name, email address and profile picture. They are used only to identify you in the app and to show your name to other meeting participants.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Collab requests access to your calendar events (calendar.events) so that it can keep your Collab meetings and your Google Calendar in sync in both directions: meetings scheduled in Collab are added to Google Calendar, and events created or changed in Google Calendar appear in Collab.

To keep the sync running without asking you to sign in again, the Collab server stores a Google refresh token for your account, encrypted. It is never shared with anyone and is used only to read and write your calendar events for this sync. You can disconnect at any time with the “Disconnect” button in Collab, which deletes the stored token and revokes access, or from your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Collab’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Meetings

Audio and video in meetings travel between participants over WebRTC. Collab does not record meetings unless you explicitly start a recording or transcript yourself.

Data deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email alexalghisi@gmail.com.
