Collab

Collab is a video meeting and collaboration app built and operated by Alessandro Alghisi. It brings meetings, scheduling, chat and a shared code editor together in one place.

Open Collab

What you can do with Collab

  • Start or join video meetings in the browser.
  • Schedule meetings and invite other people.
  • Chat and share files with participants.
  • Write and run code together in a live, shared editor during a meeting.

How Collab uses Google data

Signing in with Google gives Collab your name, email address and profile picture, used to identify you in the app and show your name to other meeting participants.

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Collab requests the calendar.events permission to keep your Collab meetings and your Google Calendar in sync in both directions. You can disconnect at any time. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
